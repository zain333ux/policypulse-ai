University Attendance Policy Proposal

Effective from the upcoming semester, all undergraduate students must maintain at least 85% attendance in every enrolled course.

Students who fail to meet the 85% attendance requirement in any course will not be allowed to sit in the final examination for that course.

Attendance will be recorded by course instructors during each class session. Students are responsible for monitoring their own attendance status throughout the semester.

The policy is intended to improve classroom participation, academic discipline, and student performance.

Students falling below the required attendance percentage may be marked as ineligible for final exams.

The university may review attendance records before final examination clearance is issued.

This policy applies to all undergraduate students across all departments.

The policy does not clearly define a medical exemption process, appeal procedure, disability accommodation process, working student flexibility, transport-related exceptions, or a formal attendance dispute resolution method.
