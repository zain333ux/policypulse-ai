# PolicyPulse AI Demo Video Script

Target length: 90–120 seconds.

## Recording sequence

1. Open the landing page and state the problem:
   “Public feedback is collected, but decision-makers struggle to connect it
   back to the policy and the people who submitted it.”
2. Select **Load verified sample** and briefly show the attendance policy and
   ten stakeholder comments.
3. Select **Analyze policy** and show the real stage progress.
4. On **Overview**, highlight sentiment, critical gaps, and the concern chart.
5. Select an evidence badge such as `COM-001` and show the exact source text.
6. Open **Policy gaps**, then **Recommendations**, emphasizing traceability and
   revised wording.
7. Open **Executive memo** and download the PDF report.
8. Close with:
   “PolicyPulse turns unstructured consultation data into an evidence-first,
   reviewable policy brief—without hiding the AI’s sources or limitations.”

## Recording checklist

- Use a clean browser profile at 1440×900.
- Hide bookmarks and unrelated tabs.
- Use the sample dataset so the flow is deterministic.
- Keep zoom at 100%.
- Record both cursor and voice.
- Add captions and export at 1080p.
- Place the final video URL in `README.md`.
