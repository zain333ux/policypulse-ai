RE: Student Attendance Policy 2025 — Public Consultation Results The proposed Student Attendance Policy 2025 received Strong Support (68%) across 1,284 public comments, indicating broad community alignment with the policy's core objectives.

However, analysis reveals 7 critical gaps requiring immediate attention before implementation. The most pressing concern — raised by 34% of respondents — is the absence of a clear implementation timeline, which risks eroding public trust if not addressed.
